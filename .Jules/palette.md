## 2026-08-02 - Dropdown Menu Accessibility Pattern
**Learning:** Custom dropdown components built without underlying accessible libraries (like Radix UI) often lack proper ARIA linkage and keyboard interaction out-of-the-box. When modifying or maintaining such menus in this application, it's critical to ensure they communicate their state using `aria-expanded`, are explicitly linked to their content via dynamically generated IDs and `aria-controls` (using `useId`), and natively support closing via the `Escape` key. Furthermore, focus must be returned to the trigger element when closed via keyboard to maintain a coherent focus order.
**Action:** When creating or reviewing custom dropdowns or popovers in this project, explicitly check for: 1. `aria-expanded` on the toggle, 2. `aria-controls` on the toggle linked to the dropdown's `id`, 3. an `Escape` keydown listener to close the menu, and 4. a `useRef` call returning focus to the toggle button upon closure.
